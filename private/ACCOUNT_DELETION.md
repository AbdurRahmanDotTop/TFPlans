# Account and Data Deletion Instructions

As per Google Play policies, TF Plans allows you to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

## How to Delete Your Account In-App
1. Open the **TF Plans** application on your device.
2. Tap on the **Profile** icon to open the Profile Settings.
3. Scroll down and tap the **Delete Account** button.
4. Confirm your action when prompted. 
5. All your data (including local notes, cloud-synced notes on Firestore, and Firebase Authentication records) will be permanently deleted.

## How to Request Deletion via Email
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

1. Send an email to `[Insert Developer Email]`.
2. Subject: **Account Deletion Request - TF Plans**
3. In the email body, please provide the email address associated with your Google Sign-In for TF Plans.
4. We will verify your request and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.

*Note: Once deleted, your notes and data cannot be recovered.*
