# Privacy Policy for TF Plans

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

Techily Fly ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by TF Plans.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide better services to our users:
*   **Account Information:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture to create and manage your account.
*   **User Content:** Notes, checklists, images, and audio you create within the app.
*   **Usage Data & Device Information:** We may collect information about your device, IP address, and how you interact with the app.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
*   To provide, maintain, and improve our services (including syncing your notes across devices via Firebase Firestore).
*   To personalize your experience.
*   To provide AI-generated content (using Google Generative AI / Gemini API).
*   To serve personalized advertisements (using Google AdMob).

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Link to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:
*   [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)
*   [Google AdMob](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
*   [Google Generative AI](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Data Storage and Security
Your notes are stored locally on your device and securely synced to Firebase Firestore if you are logged in. Data is encrypted in transit. We implement standard security measures to protect your data.

## 5. Account Deletion and Data Retention
You have the right to delete your account and all associated data. You can do this directly within the app by navigating to **Profile > Delete Account**. Upon deletion, all your synced notes and account information will be permanently removed from our servers.

## 6. Children's Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
